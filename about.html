For Freeman café culture is a way to connect

Born in Singapore to Hong Kong parents Freeman is a teenager with an idea: make Caffeine a place where coffee starts a conversation

As café culture travels the world his vision stays personal Different backgrounds a shared table and something to talk about

My Home Flavor

Being born in Singapore and having Hong Kong parents gives Freeman two places to draw from

That connection shows up on the Caffeine menu: kaya toast beside Hong Kong milk tea and French toast with syrup

For someone who knows these flavours it can feel like home For someone trying them for the first time it can be the start of a conversation

Why a café

Because spending time together can begin with something as simple as choosing a drink

Freeman wants Caffeine to make room for the everyday moments: catching up with a friend taking a break or getting to know someone new

His hope is that people from different backgrounds can find something familiar here and leave with something new to share

The idea is still growing The part he wants to keep is simple: everyone should feel welcome at the table
